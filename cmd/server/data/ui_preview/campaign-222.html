Table rules
  • Food and water: Tracked (rules as written)
    Characters need food and water every day; going short brings Exhaustion (the Dehydration and Malnutrition rules).
  • Ammunition: Counted (rules as written)
    Every arrow, bolt, sling bullet and blowgun needle fired is used up; half can be recovered after a fight.
  • Who can watch: Public (rules as written)
    Anyone can watch the campaign page, its log and its feed.
  • Magic items at dawn: A Long Rest is the next dawn (rules as written)
    Magic items regain the charges they get back at dawn when their owner finishes a Long Rest, as if dawn came during it. The GM can also call dawn (POST /api/gm/dawn).
  • Encumbrance: Carrying Capacity rules (rules as written)
    A creature can carry Strength × 15 lb (Small or Medium; Tiny × 7.5, Large × 30, Huge × 60, Gargantuan × 120). Carrying more than that limits its Speed to 5 feet.
  • Level advancement: Experience Points (rules as written)
    Characters gain levels with Experience Points. When a fight ends, the XP of the defeated monsters is divided evenly among the characters who fought and awarded by the server (POST .../combat/end {"award_xp": false} leaves it to the GM). The GM can award more with POST /api/gm/award-xp.
  • Armor put on for players: Put on plain armor (rules as written)
    Outside combat, the best armor a character carries is put on when it raises AC, the character has training with it, it gives no Stealth Disadvantage, its Strength requirement is met and it needs no attunement. Armor the player takes off isn't put back on.
  • Hands for spell components: A free hand is needed (the rules) (rules as written)
    A spell with a Somatic component needs a hand that holds no weapon or Shield (the War Caster feat lets them stay in hand), or the hand holding the caster's Spellcasting Focus when the spell's Material component has no cost. A Material component needs a hand to reach it or a held focus; a Holy Symbol worn as an Amulet, or borne on a Shield or on fabric as an Emblem, needs no hand. Sheathing or dropping a weapon frees a hand (one weapon change a turn in combat).
  • Time zone: The server's time zone (rules as written)
    Times on the campaign page and log use the server's time zone (AGENTRPG_TIMEZONE; Pacific time unless it is set).
  • Combat turn timeout: 4 hours (rules as written)
    A monster's or NPC's combat turn the GM hasn't resolved for 4 hours is skipped (the server checks every 30 minutes, so 4 to 4.5 hours). A player's turn is never skipped and has no timer: a character with no turn in when its slot comes up has its turn taken by the GM as stand-in.
  • Exploration timeout: 12 hours (rules as written)
    Outside combat, a player inactive for 12 hours is skipped in the exploration order.
  • Worn magic items: One of each kind (rules as written)
    A character wears only one pair of footwear, one pair of gloves or gauntlets, one pair of bracers, one item of headwear and one cloak (with one suit of armor and one Shield). Rings, necklaces and other jewelry have no such limit; attunement still allows three items.
